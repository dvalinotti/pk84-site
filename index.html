PK84: VGC Damage Calculator

Damage and speed for Pokémon Champions, on iPhone and iPad.

PK84 shows how much damage each move does between your team and the Pokémon you expect to face, and which Pokémon moves first. Set the weather, terrain, stat stages, status and other battle conditions, and the numbers update as you go. At larger sizes, one Pokémon's matchups against a whole team show at once.

Build your team by hand or import it from a Pokémon Showdown export or a pokepast.es link. Your teams, boxes of opponents and saved sets stay on your device.
