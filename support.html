Support

Get in touch

Found a bug, a number that looks wrong, or have an idea? Email dvalinotti@icloud.com. For a wrong number, include both Pokémon's sets and the move, or a pokepast.es link, so the matchup can be reproduced.

How-tos

Import a team

Tap the team button at the top of the screen to open your saved teams, then tap Import. Paste a Pokémon Showdown export or a pokepast.es link, choose your side or Opponents, and save. Stat points go on the EVs line, 0 to 32 each.

Your team and Opponents

Your team holds one to six Pokémon. Opponents is a box of any length, for the Pokémon you expect to face. Each side is saved separately, and you can load a saved team or box onto it from the saved teams screen.

Change a Pokémon's set or conditions

Tap a Pokémon's set button to edit its set. Its status and current HP are on its condition badge, and its stat stages are on the stat rows of its set editor. Hold a move card to replace that move.

Weather, terrain and speed

Pick the weather and terrain from the toolbar. The speed list puts both teams in order by Speed, with each Pokémon's stat stage and Choice Scarf, and each team's Tailwind.

Delete your data

Delete a saved team, box or set from the saved teams screen. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Questions

Why does a number differ from another calculator?

PK84's damage math is a port of the NCP VGC Damage Calculator's Pokémon Champions code, and it matches that calculator's results, including the few places where it differs from the game. If PK84 and that calculator disagree, please email the sets and the move.

Does PK84 work offline?

Yes. Only importing a pokepast.es link needs a connection.

Is PK84 official?

No. PK84 is an unofficial, fan-made app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo, Creatures, GAME FREAK or The Pokémon Company.
