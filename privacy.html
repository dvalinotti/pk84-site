Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

In short: PK84 doesn't collect, share or sell any data about you. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your teams and settings stay on your device.

This policy covers the PK84: VGC Damage Calculator app for iPhone and iPad ("PK84"), made by Dan Valinotti, an independent developer.

What stays on your device

The teams, boxes and sets you build or import, and your settings, are stored only on your device. They are never sent to the developer or to anyone else. Deleting PK84 deletes them. Like the rest of your apps' data, they're included in your device's iCloud or computer backups if you have backups turned on, which Apple and your device settings control.

Importing a pokepast.es link

The only time PK84 connects to the internet is when you import a team from a pokepast.es link. PK84 then asks pokepast.es for that one paste. As with any website you visit, pokepast.es receives your IP address with the request; how it handles that is up to pokepast.es. PK84 sends nothing else. Pasted Showdown text is read on your device and never leaves it.

Information Apple shares with developers

  • If you have turned on Share With App Developers (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with the developer. Apple collects these, not PK84, and they don't identify you.
  • If you test PK84 through TestFlight, Apple shares with the developer what TestFlight's terms describe, such as your name, email address, the feedback and screenshots you send, and crash reports.

This information is used only to fix problems and improve PK84.

If you email the developer

Your email address and message are used only to reply to you and to fix what you report. They aren't shared or added to any list.

Children

PK84 doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
